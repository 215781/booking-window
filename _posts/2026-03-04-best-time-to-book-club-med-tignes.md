Club Med Tignes sits at 2,100m in Val Claret, at the top of the Espace Killy, the ski area it shares with Val d'Isère. With the Grande Motte glacier above it, it has one of the longest and most reliable seasons in Club Med's Alps range, running from early December to the end of April.

We have checked its prices every morning since April 2026. Here is what every week of the 2026/27 season costs, and what that means for when to book.

> **The short answer:** at Tignes, **late April** is the lowest-priced time to go (£6,314 for a family of four), and **Easter 2027** costs much less than the winter peak (£8,874 for the first Easter week). **February half-term** (£13,088) carries a small premium over January by Tignes standards, and **New Year** (£13,462) is the most expensive family week. Most prices have held since April, so there has been little reward for waiting.

## What the numbers show

- **The season peaks at New Year and half-term.** For a family, New Year (£13,462) and the two February weeks (about £13,090) are the top of the range.
- **January is flat.** Every Sunday from 3 to 31 January was £11,708 for a family. There is no "quiet" January week that costs less.
- **The half-term premium is modest here.** Half-term was 12% above January at Tignes, compared with 30-40% at resorts like Valmorel and La Rosière. If you are tied to half-term, Tignes holds its value better than its headline price suggests.
- **Easter is the standout school holiday.** The first Easter week (28 March) was £8,874 for a family, £4,214 less than half-term and less than any week in mid-March.
- **Late April is the lowest.** The final weeks fall to £6,314-£7,958 for a family, with the glacier keeping the upper slopes skiable.
- **One odd week:** 21 March showed £12,268 for a family, well above the weeks either side. Jumps like this usually mean the lower-priced room types for that week have sold, not that the whole week has been repriced.

## What prices have done since April

We first priced these weeks on 26 April 2026. By 1 October, for a family of four:

- **11 weeks** were unchanged, **8** had risen and **2** had fallen.
- The biggest fall was the week of 7 February, from £16,224 to £13,086. The first Easter week also eased, from £9,754 to £8,874.
- Rises were mostly small (for example 31 January, £11,180 to £11,708), apart from the 21 March jump above.

For Tignes, that adds up to a resort where prices are set early and rarely fall. Waiting has not been rewarded on most weeks.

## When to book Club Med Tignes

- **School holidays (Christmas, New Year, half-term):** book once you have chosen your week. Prices for these weeks have held or risen since April, and family rooms are what sell out first.
- **Easter 2027:** the best value school holiday at Tignes, and the altitude makes late March a safe bet for snow. There is less pressure on these weeks, so you have time to compare. Our [Easter 2027 guide](/blog/easter-2027-ski-holidays-prices/) compares every resort.
- **Flexible dates:** early December and late April are the lowest-priced weeks of the season. Early December depends on early snowfall, while late April is spring skiing, with soft snow in the afternoons.

## Tignes or Val d'Isère?

The two resorts share the Espace Killy, so the skiing is essentially the same. The prices are not. For New Year a family paid £13,462 at Tignes against £24,500 at Val d'Isère, and for half-term £13,088 against £23,846. Unless you specifically want Val d'Isère's village, Tignes gives you the same mountain for much less.
