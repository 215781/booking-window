Club Med Les Arcs Panorama sits above Arc 1600, in the Paradiski area that links Les Arcs with La Plagne. It is one of the easiest Club Med resorts to reach by train, thanks to the funicular from Bourg-Saint-Maurice, and its terrain suits mixed-ability families well.

We have checked its prices every morning since April 2026. Les Arcs has behaved differently from most resorts: more weeks have fallen in price than risen.

> **The short answer:** at Les Arcs, **mid-April** is the lowest-priced time to go (£5,348 for a family of four on 18 April), and **Easter 2027** is good value (£8,674 and £7,170 for the two Easter weeks). **New Year** is very expensive this season (£17,912 for a family). Most weeks have **fallen** since April, by a median of 6%, so this is one resort where watching the price has paid.

## What the numbers show

- **New Year stands out.** At £17,912 for a family (£11,602 for two adults), New Year costs over £6,900 more than Christmas week. If you want a festive week here, Christmas is much better value.
- **The week before half-term costs more than half-term.** 7 February (£13,424) is the Welsh half-term and the start of the Paris school holidays, and it is priced above the English half-term week (£12,384).
- **Watch the odd January week.** 24 January showed £14,550 for a family when the weeks around it were about £9,200-£10,200. A jump like that usually means the lower-priced room types have sold for that week.
- **Easter is the best-value school holiday.** The two Easter weeks were £8,674 and £7,170, against £12,384 for half-term.
- **Spring is lowest.** From 11 April prices fall to £6,336 and then £5,348 for the final week.

## What prices have done since April

We first priced these weeks on 26 April 2026. By 1 October, for a family of four:

- **14 of 19 weeks were lower**, 3 were higher and 2 unchanged. The median change was -6%.
- The biggest falls were 28 February (£11,626 to £9,976) and 31 January (£10,686 to £9,232).
- The biggest rises were New Year (£13,152 to £17,912) and 24 January (£10,686 to £14,550).

So at Les Arcs, the quieter weeks have eased while the most in-demand week, New Year, has climbed sharply.

## When to book Club Med Les Arcs

- **New Year:** if you are set on it, do not expect it to fall back; it has risen by £4,760 since April. Christmas week is the better festive option here.
- **Half-term:** prices have barely moved (£12,208 in April, £12,384 now). Book when you are ready, and choose the English half-term week rather than the one before it.
- **Term-time and spring weeks:** these have tended to drift down, so there is a case for watching before you book, as long as you are flexible on room type.
- **Easter 2027:** good value and less pressured. See our [Easter 2027 guide](/blog/easter-2027-ski-holidays-prices/).

## Les Arcs or Peisey-Vallandry?

Both Club Med resorts ski the Paradiski area. Peisey-Vallandry sits on the Vanoise Express side, nearer La Plagne; Les Arcs is above Arc 1600, closest to the funicular. For a family, New Year at Peisey-Vallandry was £10,490 against £17,912 at Les Arcs, while half-term was similar (£12,184 against £12,384).
