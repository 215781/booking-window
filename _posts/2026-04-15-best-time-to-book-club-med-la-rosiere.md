Club Med La Rosière sits at 1,850m on the sunny side of the Tarentaise, with skiing that links across the border to La Thuile in Italy through the Espace San Bernardo. It is a quieter, family-focused resort, and its season runs from mid-December to mid-April.

We have checked its prices every morning since April 2026. La Rosière is one of only two resorts we track where most prices have fallen.

> **The short answer:** at La Rosière, the **first and last weeks** of the season are the lowest-priced (£6,728 on 13 December, £6,116 on 11 April, for a family of four). **February half-term** (£11,900) carries one of the steepest premiums in the range, about 43% above January. **Easter 2027** (£7,260) is good value. Most prices have **fallen** since April, so watching before you book has paid here.

## What the numbers show

- **Half-term is the expensive week.** At £11,900 for a family it matches New Year and is about 43% above the lowest January week. At Alpe d'Huez or Tignes the half-term premium is 8-12%.
- **The week before half-term is cheaper here.** 7 February (£10,648) was £1,252 less than half-term, unlike several other resorts.
- **January varies.** The family price ranged from £8,318 (3 and 31 January) to £9,694 (10 and 17 January).
- **Easter is good value.** The first Easter week was £7,260 and the second £6,958, both well below half-term.
- **The season's bookends are lowest.** 13 December (£6,728) and 11 April (£6,116).

## What prices have done since April

We first priced these weeks on 26 April 2026. By 1 October, for a family of four:

- **14 of 18 weeks were lower**, 3 were unchanged and 1 was higher. The median change was about -10%.
- Examples: 3 January fell from £9,694 to £8,318, and 7 February from £11,760 to £10,648.
- Half-term (£11,900) has not moved.

## When to book Club Med La Rosière

- **Half-term:** the price has held since April while other weeks fell. If half-term is fixed for you, compare it with resorts that have a smaller premium, such as Alpe d'Huez, Tignes or Val Thorens.
- **Christmas and New Year:** Christmas (£9,860) is around £2,000 less than New Year for a family.
- **Term-time weeks and spring:** these have tended to drift down, so there is a case for watching before booking.
- **Easter 2027:** a strong option. See our [Easter 2027 guide](/blog/easter-2027-ski-holidays-prices/).
