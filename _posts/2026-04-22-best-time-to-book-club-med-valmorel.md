Club Med Valmorel is set in a traditional, car-free Savoyard village at 1,400m, skiing the Grand Domaine with Saint-François-Longchamp. It is one of the most family-friendly resorts in the range, and one of the more affordable outside the school holidays. Its season is short: 20 December to the end of March.

We have checked its prices every morning since April 2026. Valmorel is the resort where waiting has cost the most.

> **The short answer:** at Valmorel, **every week we track has gone up since April**, by a median of 16% for a family of four. **February half-term** (£11,810) is about 40% above January. The best-value week is the last one, **28 March**, which is also the first week of the English Easter holidays (£7,880). If you want Valmorel, book rather than wait.

## What the numbers show

- **Half-term is the peak.** £11,810 for a family, the most expensive week of Valmorel's season and about 40% above the lowest January week.
- **Christmas is relatively good value.** £9,564 for a family, among the lower Christmas prices in the range.
- **January splits in two.** The first three Sundays were £8,458-£8,876; 24 and 31 January were about £1,000-£1,500 more.
- **The last week is the lowest.** 28 March (£7,880) is the first week of the English Easter holidays, so it is the best-value school-holiday week at Valmorel.
- **It is a short season.** There are no departures before 20 December or after 28 March.

## What prices have done since April

We first priced these weeks on 26 April 2026. By 1 October, **all 15 weeks were higher** for a family of four, with a median rise of 16%:

- 24 January: £7,554 to £9,984 (+£2,430)
- 31 January: £7,554 to £9,680 (+£2,126)
- 21 March: £6,504 to £8,402 (+£1,898)
- Half-term: £10,742 to £11,810 (+£1,068)

No other resort we track has risen across the board like this. It suggests strong demand for a resort with a limited number of family rooms.

## When to book Club Med Valmorel

- **Any week:** the evidence says book once you have chosen. Prices have only gone one way since April.
- **Half-term:** with a 40% premium, compare it with resorts where half-term costs relatively less, such as Alpe d'Huez, Val Thorens or La Plagne 2100.
- **Easter:** 28 March is Valmorel's best value and fits the English Easter holidays. Note there is no second Easter week here. See our [Easter 2027 guide](/blog/easter-2027-ski-holidays-prices/).
