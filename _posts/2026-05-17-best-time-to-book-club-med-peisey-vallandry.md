Club Med Peisey-Vallandry sits among the forests on the La Plagne side of the Paradiski area, close to the Vanoise Express cable car that links La Plagne and Les Arcs. It suits families with mixed abilities: gentle tree-lined runs near the resort, and one of the biggest linked ski areas in the Alps above it. The season runs from mid-December to mid-April.

We have checked its prices every morning since April 2026.

> **The short answer:** at Peisey-Vallandry, **New Year (£10,490) is slightly cheaper than Christmas (£10,580)** for a family of four this season, which is unusual. **February half-term** (£12,184) is about 40% above January. **Easter 2027** (£7,604 and £7,326) and the **final week** (£6,778) are the best value.

## What the numbers show

- **New Year for less than Christmas.** At almost every other resort New Year is the dearer week. Here it is £90 less for a family, after New Year fell and Christmas rose.
- **Half-term is the peak.** £12,184 for a family, about 40% above the lowest January week (£8,704 on 31 January).
- **Late February and early March stay high.** 28 February (£11,714) and 7 March (£11,256) are priced close to half-term, while 21 February (£10,194) is lower.
- **Easter and mid-April are the value weeks.** The two Easter weeks were £7,604 and £7,326, and the final week (11 April) £6,778.

## What prices have done since April

We first priced these weeks on 26 April 2026. By 1 October, for a family of four, 9 weeks were unchanged, 7 had risen and 2 had fallen:

- **Rises:** 7 March from £8,674 to £11,256, half-term from £10,602 to £12,184, and Christmas from £9,208 to £10,580.
- **Falls:** New Year from £12,054 to £10,490, and 31 January from £10,000 to £8,704.

## When to book Club Med Peisey-Vallandry

- **Half-term:** it has risen by £1,582 since April. If you are set on it, book; if not, compare with resorts where the half-term premium is smaller.
- **Christmas or New Year:** New Year is the slightly better value this season, which is rare. Check both before you choose.
- **Easter 2027:** good value, and the season runs to mid-April. See our [Easter 2027 guide](/blog/easter-2027-ski-holidays-prices/).

## Peisey-Vallandry or Les Arcs?

Both Club Med resorts ski Paradiski. For New Year a family paid £10,490 here against £17,912 at [Les Arcs Panorama](/blog/best-time-to-book-club-med-les-arcs/); at half-term the two were similar (£12,184 and £12,384). Les Arcs is easier by train (funicular from Bourg-Saint-Maurice); Peisey-Vallandry is reached from Landry station.
