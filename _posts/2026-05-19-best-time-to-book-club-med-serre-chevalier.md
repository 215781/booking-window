Club Med Serre-Chevalier is in the Hautes-Alpes, near the old fortified town of Briançon, further south than the Savoie resorts. The Serre Chevalier valley gets more sunshine than the northern Alps and has a big, varied ski area with tree-lined runs that help in poor visibility. The season runs from 20 December to early April, with both Saturday and Sunday starts on some weeks.

We have checked its prices every morning since May 2026.

> **The short answer:** at Serre-Chevalier, **Easter 2027** is the best-value time to go (£7,688-£8,038 for a family of four). **February half-term** (£11,508) is cheaper than the week before it (£12,358). Prices have **only gone up** since May, by a median of 13%, so book rather than wait.

## What the numbers show

- **The week before half-term is the peak.** 7 February (£12,358) is the Welsh half-term and the start of the Paris holidays. The English half-term week was £850 less.
- **Half-term premium is modest.** Half-term was about 15% above the lowest-priced January week after schools go back (£10,032 on 24 January).
- **New Year costs more than Christmas.** £12,052 against £10,392 for a family.
- **March and Easter are the value weeks.** From 7 March the family price falls below £9,000, and the two Easter weeks are the lowest of the season.

## What prices have done since May

We first priced these weeks on 7 May 2026. By 1 October, for a family of four, **16 departures had risen, 6 were unchanged and none had fallen**:

- 10 and 31 January both rose from £8,132 to £10,506 (+£2,374).
- New Year rose from £10,372 to £12,052.
- The March and Easter weeks have held.

## When to book Club Med Serre-Chevalier

- **January, New Year and half-term:** these are the weeks that have risen. Book once you have decided.
- **Easter 2027:** the lowest prices of the season and both Easter weeks are available (the season ends on 4 April). See our [Easter 2027 guide](/blog/easter-2027-ski-holidays-prices/).
- **Getting there:** the nearest airports are Turin and Grenoble, and the train runs to Briançon. It is further from the UK's usual ski airports than the Savoie resorts, so price the travel in.
