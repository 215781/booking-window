La Plagne 2100 is the resort that started this site. Two families met there having booked the same week at the same resort, and one had paid £1,600 more than the other. The difference was when they booked.

The resort itself sits at 2,100m above the main La Plagne villages, ski-in, ski-out, at the heart of the Paradiski area that links La Plagne with Les Arcs. It is one of the highest Club Med resorts in the Alps, which makes it a safe bet for snow at both ends of the season.

We have checked its prices every morning since June 2026 (when we corrected the resort code we were tracking).

> **The short answer:** La Plagne 2100 is the **best value for school holidays** in Club Med's Alps range this season: the **lowest February half-term** (£9,784 for a family of four) and the **lowest New Year** (£9,844) of the 11 resorts we track, despite its altitude. Prices have been **very stable** since June. **Mid-December**, **Easter** and **mid-April** are the lowest-priced weeks.

## What the numbers show

- **The lowest school-holiday prices in the range.** Half-term (£9,784) and New Year (£9,844) were the lowest family prices for those weeks at any Club Med Alps resort we track. Christmas (£9,172) was second only to Grand Massif.
- **A moderate half-term premium.** Half-term was about 26% above the January weeks (£7,742).
- **One odd January week.** 31 January jumped to £11,460 for a family, while two adults still paid £5,176. A gap like that usually means the family room types for that week are running out.
- **Spring is the lowest.** Easter week 1 (£7,101), Easter week 2 (£6,724) and the final week, 11 April (£6,060).

## What prices have done since June

By 1 October, 15 of the 18 weeks were exactly where they were in early June for a family of four. The changes:

- 31 January rose from £7,542 to £11,460.
- Easter week 1 rose from £6,616 to £7,101.
- New Year fell from £10,334 to £9,844.

La Plagne 2100 is the steadiest resort we track. That makes it easy to plan: the price you see today is a good guide to the price next month, except where family rooms start to run out.

## When to book Club Med La Plagne 2100

- **Half-term and New Year:** the best-value options in the range. Book once you have decided; prices are not falling.
- **Late January:** check 31 January carefully; the weeks either side are far lower.
- **Easter 2027:** good value at altitude. See our [Easter 2027 guide](/blog/easter-2027-ski-holidays-prices/).

## La Plagne or Val d'Isère?

Both are high, ski-in, ski-out resorts with huge linked ski areas. The difference is price: at New Year a family paid £9,844 here against £24,500 at Val d'Isère. Our [La Plagne vs Val d'Isère comparison](/blog/club-med-val-disere-vs-la-plagne/) goes through it week by week.
