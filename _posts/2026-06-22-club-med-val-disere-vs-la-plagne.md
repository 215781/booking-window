Club Med Val d'Isère and La Plagne 2100 are two of the brand's highest French Alps resorts, both ski-in, ski-out, both on huge linked ski areas. On paper they are close. On price, for a family in the school holidays, they are not even in the same league.

> **The short answer:** for school holidays, **La Plagne 2100 is less than half the price** of Val d'Isère for a family of four (£9,784 against £23,846 at half-term). Choose **Val d'Isère** if you are strong skiers who want the Espace Killy and can book very early. Choose **La Plagne 2100** for almost everything else, including mixed-ability families.

## The two resorts

**Club Med Val d'Isère** is at 1,850m in the Espace Killy, shared with Tignes. It is Club Med's most premium resort in the Alps, in one of the best-known ski towns in France, with steep, challenging terrain.

**Club Med La Plagne 2100** sits at 2,100m above the main La Plagne villages, at the centre of Paradiski, which links La Plagne with Les Arcs. The skiing is huge and varied, with plenty of gentle runs for learners and improvers.

- **The gap is biggest in the school holidays**: £14,062 for a family at half-term and £14,656 at New Year.
- **It narrows in spring**: about £2,500 for a family by mid-April.
- **Val d'Isère's Christmas week was already unavailable for families** by 1 October.

## How prices have moved

**Val d'Isère** has moved a lot since we started tracking in April 2026, mostly upwards for families: half-term from £15,450 to £23,846, and 21 March from £10,932 to £18,542. Jumps like these usually mean the lower-priced rooms have sold.

**La Plagne 2100** has barely moved: 15 of its 18 weeks were unchanged between June and October. The one big change was 31 January, which rose to £11,460 for a family.

## Which should you book?

**Book Val d'Isère if:** everyone in your group is a strong skier who wants the Espace Killy, you value the town itself, and you can book a peak week as early as possible.

**Book La Plagne 2100 if:** you want high-altitude, ski-in, ski-out skiing at a far lower price, you have mixed abilities, or you want a school-holiday week that is still available at a stable price.

If you are drawn to the Espace Killy but not Val d'Isère's prices, look at [Club Med Tignes](/blog/best-time-to-book-club-med-tignes/): same ski area, £13,088 for half-term.
