Club Med Alpe d'Huez and Val Thorens Sensations sit in the same part of the price range and are often on the same shortlist. For the main school holidays they are almost level on price. Where they differ is in the shoulder weeks, and in what you get on the mountain.

> **The short answer:** for **Christmas and February half-term**, the two cost almost the same for a family of four (within £140). **Alpe d'Huez** is better value at **Easter** and costs more at **New Year**. **Val Thorens** is much dearer in **March** but has the best snow and the latest season, to the end of April.

## The two resorts

**Club Med Alpe d'Huez** is at 1,860m on the sunny side of the Alpe d'Huez ski area, around 250km of pistes, with long runs that suit intermediates and a lively resort. Its season runs to mid-April.

**Club Med Val Thorens Sensations** is at 2,300m, the highest Club Med resort in the Alps, in Les 3 Vallées, the largest linked ski area in the world. The snow is as reliable as it gets in the Alps, and its season runs to late April.

- **Christmas and half-term are level.** The difference was under £140 for a family.
- **New Year favours Val Thorens** by £2,144 for a family.
- **The week before half-term favours Val Thorens** by £3,160. At Alpe d'Huez that week (Welsh half-term, Paris holidays) is the dearest of the season.
- **March favours Alpe d'Huez** by a long way: £3,466 less for a family on 7 March. Val Thorens' March weeks have risen sharply since May.
- **Couples see a different picture.** For two adults, Val Thorens was much dearer in early December and at Easter week 1, a sign of which room types are left.

## How prices have moved

At **Val Thorens**, 8 of 21 weeks have risen since May and none have fallen, with the biggest rises in March. At **Alpe d'Huez**, 9 weeks have risen and 3 fallen since April; New Year and the week before half-term climbed, while half-term itself eased from £12,156 to £11,054.

## Which should you book?

**Book Alpe d'Huez if:** you want Easter or March at a lower price, your group are intermediates who like long sunny runs, or you want a livelier resort.

**Book Val Thorens if:** snow reliability matters most, you want the scale of Les 3 Vallées, you are going at New Year or the week before half-term, or you want to ski in late April.
