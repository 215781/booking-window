Two dates matter this autumn if you are planning a family beach holiday for summer 2027:

- **13 October 2026:** Club Med opens its sale for May to November 2027.
- **2 November 2026:** Mark Warner's "Super Early Bird" prices for summer 2027 end.

Both companies want you to book now. We have a full summer of daily price data from 2026 and three months of Mark Warner 2027 prices, so here is what the numbers say about whether you should.

> **The short answer:** if you want a **Club Med Mediterranean resort in the school holidays**, book early: in summer 2026 those prices rose by a median of 24% and a fifth of weeks sold out for families. For **Mark Warner**, the early-bird deadline is real but the evidence is mixed: its 2027 prices have barely moved since July, and in 2026 late prices fell. Book early if you need a specific week, room or flight; otherwise watch the price.

## What each company is offering

**Club Med (sale from 13 October 2026):** Club Med says early bookers can get up to 20% off summer 2027, and that the offer covers summer weeks, school half-terms and family rooms. Children under four stay free. Pre-registration is open before the sale.

**Mark Warner (early bird until 2 November 2026):** Mark Warner says its Super Early Bird prices end on 2 November, after which prices increase. It also offers £50 per person off for paying in full (code PAYNOW50), which it says can be combined with the early-bird price.

Two cautions, from watching these offers for a year:

- **"Up to" means the best case.** The biggest percentages usually apply to quieter weeks and pricier rooms, not the school-holiday family room you actually need.
- **A percentage off is measured against the company's own reference price.** What matters is the price you pay compared with what the same week costs later. That is what we track. Our [guide to Club Med's early booking offer](/blog/club-med-early-booking-offer-how-it-works/) goes into this in more detail.

## What happened last summer

We checked every summer-holiday week in 2026 every morning until departure ([full analysis here](/blog/summer-2026-holiday-prices-did-waiting-pay-off/)). The two companies went in opposite directions:

- **Club Med, Mediterranean and North Africa:** family prices rose by a median of **24%** between mid-May and departure, and about **one in five** weeks that were bookable in early summer had sold out for families by the final week. The biggest rises were at Bodrum, Magna Marbella and Da Balaia.
- **Club Med, long-haul:** roughly flat (median -1%).
- **Mark Warner:** family prices (with flights) fell by a median of **21%** in the final weeks, for 31 of 33 departures.

One summer is not a law of nature. But it is a lot more than most booking advice is based on.

## So when should you book?

**Book in Club Med's sale if:**

- you want a Mediterranean or North African resort in July or August, or at a half-term;
- you need a family room or connecting rooms;
- your dates are fixed by the school calendar.

That is the combination where prices rose and weeks sold out in 2026.

**Think about watching rather than booking now if:**

- you are looking at Mark Warner and could take any of several weeks or resorts;
- you are looking at Club Med's long-haul resorts in the summer, which were flat in 2026;
- you would be comfortable booking in the spring or later if the price drops.

**Either way:**

1. Note the price of your week on the day you look. It is the only way to know later whether you did well.
2. Check the cancellation and amendment terms. A flexible booking at a fair price beats a rigid one at a slightly lower price.
3. Set an alert. We check both companies every morning and will email you if your week moves.
