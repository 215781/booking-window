February half-term is the ski week most British families can actually take, which is exactly why it is so expensive. In 2027 it also lands on top of two French holiday zones, so the Alps will be at their busiest.

We check Club Med prices every morning at 11 French Alps resorts. Here is what half-term costs at each one, how it compares with the weeks either side, and what prices have done since the spring.

> **The short answer:** on 1 October 2026, a family of four (two adults, two children aged 4-11) was quoted **£9,784 to £23,846** for seven nights from 14 February 2027 at Club Med, without flights. **La Plagne 2100** was the least expensive and one of the highest. Half-term cost a median **30% more** than the lowest-priced January week at the same resort, and it has gone **up** at six of the 11 resorts since April.

## Half-term dates and departures

- **England:** half-term is Monday 15 to Friday 19 February 2027. Depart Saturday 13 or Sunday 14 February.
- **Wales:** 8 to 12 February. Depart 6 or 7 February.
- **Scotland:** many councils take only Monday 15 and Tuesday 16 February, so a full week usually means extra days off.

Most Club Med Alps resorts run Sunday to Sunday; Grand Massif and Serre-Chevalier also sell Saturday starts.

## Why this half-term is so busy

France staggers its winter holidays by zone. In 2027:

| French zone | Winter holidays |
|---|---|
| Zone C (Paris) | 6 - 22 February |
| Zone A (Lyon, Grenoble, Bordeaux) | 13 February - 1 March |
| Zone B (Marseille, Lille, Nantes) | 20 February - 8 March |

The English half-term week overlaps **zones A and C at the same time**, and the Welsh half-term overlaps zone C. Every family-friendly resort in the Alps is in demand that fortnight.

What stands out:

- **Most resorts sit in a tight band** of about £11,000 to £12,400 for a family. Outside that band, La Plagne is about £1,300 below, Tignes about £700 above and Val d'Isère roughly double.

- **Grand Massif had no family availability** for the half-term week when we checked.

At Alpe d'Huez, Les Arcs and Serre-Chevalier, the week before cost *more* than half-term. Elsewhere it was the same or up to about £1,250 less. And for English families it is term time, with the [fines and rules that go with that](/blog/term-time-holiday-fines-vs-school-holiday-prices/).

The week after (departing 21 February) was lower at most resorts, by a median of about £900 for a family. That only helps families with pre-school children, or schools with a later half-term.

## What prices have done since April

We have tracked the half-term week since late April (early May at Grand Massif, Serre-Chevalier and Val Thorens; June at La Plagne). For a family of four:

- **Rose at six resorts:** Val d'Isère (£15,450 to £23,846), Peisey-Vallandry (+£1,582), Valmorel (+£1,068), Val Thorens (+£530), Serre-Chevalier (+£514) and Les Arcs (+£176).
- **Held at three:** La Plagne, La Rosière and Tignes.
- **Fell at one:** Alpe d'Huez (£12,156 to £11,054).
- **Sold out for families at one:** Grand Massif.

That is the pattern we would expect for the busiest week of the season: prices rarely fall, and the lowest-priced rooms go first. Large jumps like Val d'Isère's usually mean the cheaper room types have sold, not that the same room has been repriced.

## The alternative: Easter

If half-term is not fixed for you, look at Easter 2027. It is early (Easter Sunday 28 March), comes before most French spring holidays, and in our September checks the first Easter week cost a median **32% less** than half-term at the same resort. Our [Easter 2027 ski guide](/blog/easter-2027-ski-holidays-prices/) has the resort-by-resort numbers.

## Getting there

- **Train:** Eurostar's ski service runs out on Saturday 13 February 2027 (the season's outbound trains run from 19 December to 20 February), changing at Lille for Moûtiers, Aime-la-Plagne, Landry and Bourg-Saint-Maurice. Most Club Med weeks start on a Sunday, so plan for a night in the valley or check whether a Saturday start is available.
- **Flying:** half-term Saturday is one of the busiest days of the year at Geneva and Lyon. Price your flights at the same time as the resort.

## How to book half-term well

1. **Decide your shortlist on price and premium together.** A resort with a small half-term premium, such as Alpe d'Huez, Val Thorens or Tignes, gives you more for that week.
2. **Book the room type you need sooner rather than later.** Family rooms are what sell out, and when they go the price you see jumps to the next room up.
3. **Keep watching after you book**, if your booking can be changed. Our [school holidays page](/school-holidays/) shows the half-term price at every resort, updated every morning, and we will email you if your week moves.
