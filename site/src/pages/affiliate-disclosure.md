When To Book is free to use. To keep it that way, some links on the site and in our emails are affiliate links. If you click one and book, the operator or travel agent may pay us a commission. **You pay exactly the same price.**

Commission never changes what our data says. Every resort we track is shown the same way, whether or not we earn from it, and we'll always say when a price is rising.

Where a link is an affiliate link, we label it. Questions: [admin@whentobook.co.uk](mailto:admin@whentobook.co.uk).
